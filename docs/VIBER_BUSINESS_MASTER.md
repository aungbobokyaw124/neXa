# neXa VIBER BUSINESS MASTER

Version: 1.0  
Status: OFFICIAL / MASTER  
Brand: neXa  
Tagline: Build Faster. Create Smarter.

## 1. PURPOSE
Define permanent rules for neXa Viber business communication, customer support, service announcements, promotions, automation and business messaging.

## 2. BRAND
Official brand: `neXa`  
Tagline: `Build Faster. Create Smarter.`  
Colors: `#0B1F3A`, `#10B981`, `#FFFFFF`.

## 3. BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
Use Viber Business communication to:
- support customers
- explain services
- distribute useful information
- announce genuine offers
- guide customers to verified channels
- build trust

## 4. CUSTOMER-FIRST RULE
Messages must be useful to the recipient. Avoid unnecessary promotional spam.

## 5. MESSAGE STYLE
Be:
- clear
- polite
- direct
- professional
- helpful
- mobile-friendly

## 6. LANGUAGE
Use natural Burmese for Myanmar customers. Use English when appropriate for international customers or technical material.

## 7. WELCOME MESSAGE
A welcome message should explain neXa and provide a clear next action such as viewing services, asking a question or contacting support.

## 8. BUSINESS PROFILE
Keep business name, logo, description and official links consistent with the neXa brand.

## 9. SERVICES
Service descriptions must reflect actual services. Do not promise unsupported capabilities.

## 10. PRICING
Verify current prices before publishing. Include important conditions where applicable.

## 11. OFFERS
Promotional offers must be genuine and include relevant conditions, dates and limitations.

## 12. NO FALSE URGENCY
Never invent scarcity, deadlines or emergencies.

## 13. CUSTOMER SUPPORT
Support responses should identify the problem, provide the next step and escalate when necessary.

## 14. SUPPORT HOURS
If operating hours are published, keep them current.

## 15. CUSTOMER DATA
Collect only information needed for the stated purpose.

## 16. PRIVACY
Never expose customer personal information publicly.

## 17. CREDENTIALS
Never request or store customer passwords, OTPs, private keys or API secrets through normal chat.

## 18. PAYMENT SAFETY
For payments, provide only verified official payment instructions. Never ask users to send authentication codes.

## 19. SCAMS
Warn customers about impersonation and suspicious payment requests when relevant.

## 20. LINKS
Use verified official links. Check links before publishing where possible.

## 21. AUTOMATION
Automation may be used for:
- welcome messages
- FAQs
- notifications
- support routing
- appointment/order updates
- approved campaigns

## 22. BOT SECURITY
API credentials and bot secrets must be stored securely, never in public repositories or frontend code.

## 23. API ARCHITECTURE
Preferred:
Viber → secure backend/worker → business logic/API.
Never expose private credentials to the client.

## 24. WEBHOOK SECURITY
Validate incoming webhook requests and protect endpoints against abuse.

## 25. RATE LIMITS
Respect current Viber API limits and retry policies.

## 26. ERROR HANDLING
Automation failures must be logged safely and provide a fallback where possible.

## 27. HUMAN ESCALATION
When automation cannot solve a customer issue, route to human support where available.

## 28. AI SUPPORT
If AI is connected, it must follow neXa master rules, protect privacy and clearly avoid inventing information.

## 29. AI LIMITATIONS
AI must say when it does not know something or cannot verify a current business fact.

## 30. BUSINESS CLAIMS
Do not fabricate customers, partnerships, reviews, revenue or success rates.

## 31. TESTIMONIALS
Only use genuine testimonials with appropriate permission.

## 32. CUSTOMER REVIEWS
Do not manipulate or fabricate reviews.

## 33. BROADCASTS
Broadcast messages should be relevant to the recipient audience.

## 34. FREQUENCY
Avoid excessive broadcasts. Quality and relevance matter more than volume.

## 35. SEGMENTATION
Where supported, segment audiences based on legitimate business needs and user consent/expectations.

## 36. PERSONALIZATION
Personalization must not expose sensitive information or create an uncomfortable experience.

## 37. MARKETING
Marketing messages should communicate:
- what is offered
- why it is useful
- important conditions
- clear next step

## 38. EDUCATIONAL MESSAGES
Use Viber to provide useful technology and service education when appropriate.

## 39. CUSTOMER ONBOARDING
Onboarding should be short and guide the customer through the first useful action.

## 40. FAQ
Maintain a clear FAQ covering common service questions.

## 41. ORDER/SERVICE UPDATES
If used, transactional messages should contain accurate status information.

## 42. NOTIFICATIONS
Notifications should be timely and relevant. Avoid duplicate messages.

## 43. CONTENT REPURPOSING
Adapt content from Facebook, Telegram and TikTok rather than copying platform-specific wording unchanged.

## 44. VISUALS
Visual materials must follow the neXa design system.

## 45. LOGO
Do not distort, stretch or randomly recolor the neXa logo.

## 46. MESSAGE FORMAT
Use short paragraphs, bullets and clear calls to action.

## 47. CTA
Use truthful CTAs such as:
- View Services
- Contact neXa
- Learn More
- Ask a Question

## 48. NO SPAM
Do not use repeated unsolicited promotional messages or deceptive engagement tactics.

## 49. PLATFORM COMPLIANCE
Follow current Viber policies, API requirements and applicable laws.

## 50. CURRENT INFORMATION
Verify current Viber features, limits and business capabilities before making current claims.

## 51. COPYRIGHT
Use original or properly licensed images, videos, text and other media.

## 52. SECURITY INCIDENT
If a Viber credential is exposed:
1. revoke/rotate it
2. remove it from code
3. update secrets
4. inspect recent activity
5. redeploy safely

## 53. ACCESS CONTROL
Give business account administration only to authorized personnel.

## 54. ACCOUNT SECURITY
Use available account security controls and strong authentication.

## 55. LOGGING
Logs must not contain passwords, tokens or unnecessary sensitive information.

## 56. BACKUPS
Back up important business configuration and documentation where appropriate.

## 57. CONTENT REVIEW
Before publishing:
- verify facts
- check spelling
- check links
- check branding
- check privacy
- check security

## 58. CUSTOMER TONE
Never insult, pressure or manipulate customers.

## 59. COMPLAINTS
Acknowledge complaints, identify the issue and provide a practical resolution path.

## 60. REFUNDS
Refund statements must reflect actual business policy. Do not invent policy.

## 61. SERVICE LIMITATIONS
Explain limitations when they materially affect customer expectations.

## 62. AVAILABILITY
Do not claim a service is available unless it is actually available in the customer's region/context.

## 63. REGIONAL RULES
Payment methods, features and services may differ by country. Verify regional availability.

## 64. BUSINESS HOURS
If automated support runs outside business hours, clearly distinguish automated support from human support where needed.

## 65. ESCALATION
Define internal escalation paths for technical, payment and account issues.

## 66. TECHNICAL SUPPORT
Never ask customers to perform dangerous electrical or technical work without appropriate safety warnings. For neXa digital support, provide reversible steps first.

## 67. DATA RETENTION
Keep customer data only as long as required for legitimate business purposes and applicable rules.

## 68. ANALYTICS
Use analytics to improve service quality, not to invade customer privacy.

## 69. CAMPAIGNS
Campaigns should have a clear objective, audience, start/end period and success measure.

## 70. A/B TESTING
Where available, test meaningful variations without misleading customers.

## 71. CUSTOMER FEEDBACK
Use feedback to improve products and services. Do not promise unapproved changes.

## 72. KNOWLEDGE BASE
Maintain reusable answers for common support issues.

## 73. MESSAGE TEMPLATES
Create templates for:
- welcome
- support
- order/service update
- outage
- offer
- follow-up
- feedback

## 74. OUTAGE MESSAGE
For outages:
1. acknowledge
2. state impact
3. provide workaround if verified
4. update users
5. confirm recovery

## 75. CHANGE ANNOUNCEMENT
Explain what changed, when, who is affected and what users should do.

## 76. FOLLOW-UP
Follow up only when it provides genuine value or is part of an expected service workflow.

## 77. NO FAKE AUTOMATION
Never tell customers that a human reviewed a message if it was only automated.

## 78. AI DISCLOSURE
Where appropriate, clearly identify automated/AI assistance when the distinction matters.

## 79. BUSINESS CONTINUITY
Maintain alternative support/contact methods for important customer workflows when practical.

## 80. TESTING
Before launching automation test:
- welcome flow
- buttons
- links
- webhooks
- errors
- fallback
- security

## 81. DEPLOYMENT
Deploy business automation only after testing in a safe environment where possible.

## 82. VERSION CONTROL
Keep automation code and documentation version-controlled.

## 83. DOCUMENTATION
Document:
- setup
- secrets
- endpoints
- workflows
- support process
- troubleshooting

Never document actual secret values.

## 84. QUALITY STANDARD
A good Viber Business message is short enough to read quickly but complete enough to act on.

## 85. MASTER MESSAGE TEMPLATE
```text
[neXa]

[Clear message title]

[Useful explanation]

• Key point
• Key point
• Key point

[Truthful CTA]

Official neXa channel/link
```

## 86. AI EXECUTION RULE
When asked to create Viber Business content, identify audience, purpose, message type and CTA, then produce a concise customer-friendly message following this document.

## 87. CHANGE MANAGEMENT
Major changes to this document must be versioned and approved.

## 88. FINAL BUSINESS CHECK
- [ ] Brand correct
- [ ] Facts correct
- [ ] Pricing verified if included
- [ ] Links verified
- [ ] No private data
- [ ] No secrets
- [ ] No fake claims
- [ ] CTA clear
- [ ] Customer-friendly

## 89. TRUST RULE
Customer trust is more valuable than short-term conversion.

## 90. MASTER VIBER BUSINESS PROMPT
Create Viber Business communication for neXa using the official brand identity and current project rules. Make every message useful, concise, truthful, secure and appropriate for the target customer. Never fabricate business facts, testimonials, pricing, availability or urgency.

## 91. PERMANENT PRINCIPLE
Viber Business is a customer-service and business communication channel, not a spam channel.

## 92. FINAL RULE
**Build Faster. Create Smarter.**

Serve customers honestly, protect their information and keep communication useful.

# END OF neXa VIBER BUSINESS MASTER
