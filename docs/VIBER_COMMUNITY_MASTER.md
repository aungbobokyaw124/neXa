# neXa VIBER COMMUNITY MASTER

Version: 1.0  
Status: OFFICIAL / MASTER  
Brand: neXa  
Tagline: Build Faster. Create Smarter.

## 1. PURPOSE
Define permanent rules for the neXa Viber community, including community growth, moderation, education, announcements, support and member safety.

## 2. COMMUNITY MISSION
Build a useful technology community around:
- AI
- web development
- automation
- cloud
- digital services
- education
- practical technology

## 3. BRAND
Official brand: `neXa`  
Tagline: `Build Faster. Create Smarter.`  
Colors: `#0B1F3A`, `#10B981`, `#FFFFFF`.

## 4. COMMUNITY VALUES
Members should experience:
- respect
- usefulness
- safety
- learning
- honest discussion
- practical help

## 5. MEMBER TONE
Be friendly, direct, respectful and helpful.

## 6. NO HARASSMENT
Do not allow threats, harassment, bullying or targeted abuse.

## 7. NO SPAM
Do not flood the community with repetitive advertisements, links or irrelevant messages.

## 8. NO SCAMS
Remove or escalate:
- fake investment offers
- impersonation
- phishing
- credential theft
- malicious links
- fraudulent payment requests

## 9. NO IMPERSONATION
Members must not impersonate neXa, staff, banks, governments or other organizations.

## 10. PRIVACY
Do not publish another person's private information without appropriate permission.

## 11. CREDENTIAL SAFETY
Never ask members to post:
- passwords
- OTPs
- private keys
- API tokens
- recovery codes

## 12. SECURITY EDUCATION
Security warnings should be clear and practical.

## 13. COMMUNITY RULES
Keep public rules concise. Pin important rules where the platform supports it.

## 14. WELCOME
New members should receive a clear introduction to neXa and community purpose.

## 15. INTRODUCTION
Members may introduce themselves voluntarily. Do not pressure members to disclose personal information.

## 16. DISCUSSION
Encourage useful questions and constructive answers.

## 17. DEBATE
Technical disagreements are acceptable. Personal attacks are not.

## 18. MODERATION
Moderators should act consistently and explain important moderation actions when appropriate.

## 19. MODERATOR ACCESS
Administrative access should be limited to trusted people who need it.

## 20. MODERATOR SECURITY
Use strong account security and two-factor authentication where available.

## 21. MODERATION ACTIONS
Possible actions:
- warning
- content removal
- temporary restriction
- member removal
- escalation

Use the least severe effective action when safe.

## 22. APPEALS
Where practical, members should have a way to request review of significant moderation actions.

## 23. CONTENT QUALITY
Encourage:
- tutorials
- troubleshooting
- useful tools
- project sharing
- questions
- learning resources

## 24. SELF-PROMOTION
Allow self-promotion only within clearly defined community rules and limits.

## 25. LINKS
Links should be relevant and safe. Suspicious links should be removed.

## 26. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Members may mention other tools or platforms. Do not pretend they are neXa products.

## 27. OFFICIAL LINKS
Only verified neXa links should be presented as official neXa destinations.

## 28. EDUCATION
Educational posts should prioritize practical understanding over hype.

## 29. BEGINNERS
Beginners should feel comfortable asking basic questions.

## 30. EXPERTS
Experienced members are encouraged to share knowledge without humiliating beginners.

## 31. ANSWER QUALITY
Answers should distinguish verified information from guesses or personal experience.

## 32. AI CONTENT
AI-generated answers should be checked when accuracy matters.

## 33. AI DISCLOSURE
When it materially matters, disclose that an answer or content was generated or assisted by AI.

## 34. MISINFORMATION
Correct important false technical information with evidence where possible.

## 35. NEWS
News discussion should distinguish facts, reports and opinions.

## 36. CURRENT INFORMATION
Platform features, prices and limits change. Verify current information before presenting it as current.

## 37. COPYRIGHT
Do not encourage copyright infringement. Use original or properly licensed materials.

## 38. DANGEROUS CONTENT
Do not encourage dangerous experiments or illegal activity.

## 39. TECHNICAL SAFETY
For electronics, electrical work, batteries or hardware, provide appropriate safety warnings when relevant.

## 40. MALWARE
Do not allow malware distribution or instructions intended to harm systems.

## 41. CYBERSECURITY
Security education is welcome when defensive and legitimate. Do not turn the community into a platform for abuse.

## 42. ACCOUNT RECOVERY
Never request another member's login codes or passwords.

## 43. PAYMENTS
Do not allow members to post misleading payment instructions or impersonate financial institutions.

## 44. BUSINESS PROMOTION
neXa promotional posts should provide real value and follow the business rules.

## 45. PRODUCT ANNOUNCEMENTS
Announcements must describe real features and current availability.

## 46. PRICING
Verify pricing before publishing it as current.

## 47. OFFERS
Offers must be genuine and include important conditions.

## 48. NO FAKE SCARCITY
Do not create fake urgency to pressure community members.

## 49. EVENTS
Events should clearly state topic, date/time, participation method and relevant requirements.

## 50. POLLS
Polls may be used for feedback and community research. Do not manipulate results.

## 51. FEEDBACK
Use member feedback to improve neXa products and community experience.

## 52. FEATURE REQUESTS
Feature requests are suggestions, not promises. Do not claim they are approved unless they are.

## 53. COMMUNITY CONTENT
Member-created tutorials, projects and useful resources may be highlighted with appropriate permission.

## 54. TESTIMONIALS
Do not fabricate testimonials. Use genuine member feedback only with appropriate permission.

## 55. CASE STUDIES
Clearly distinguish real case studies from hypothetical examples.

## 56. MODERATION LOGS
Keep appropriate internal moderation records without exposing unnecessary personal data.

## 57. PRIVACY OF LOGS
Moderation records should be accessible only to authorized people.

## 58. INCIDENT RESPONSE
For serious incidents:
1. secure the community
2. remove immediate threat
3. preserve relevant evidence
4. inform responsible administrators
5. take appropriate follow-up action

## 59. ACCOUNT COMPROMISE
If an admin account is compromised, secure the account, revoke unauthorized access and rotate affected credentials.

## 60. BOT SECURITY
Community bots must use minimum necessary permissions and secure tokens.

## 61. AUTOMATION
Automation may handle:
- welcome messages
- FAQs
- moderation assistance
- announcements
- reminders

Human moderators retain responsibility for important decisions.

## 62. AI MODERATION
AI may assist moderation, but high-impact moderation decisions should have human oversight where practical.

## 63. FALSE POSITIVES
Do not permanently punish members solely because an automated system may have made an error.

## 64. COMMUNITY LANGUAGE
Use clear Burmese for Myanmar members and English where appropriate. Avoid unnecessary jargon.

## 65. ACCESSIBILITY
Important community information should be readable and understandable.

## 66. CONTENT FORMATTING
Use headings, bullets and short paragraphs for long educational posts.

## 67. REPURPOSING
Content from Telegram, Facebook or TikTok may be adapted for the Viber community when useful.

## 68. CROSS-PLATFORM RULE
Do not treat every platform as identical. Adapt tone, length and CTA to Viber community use.

## 69. COMMUNITY CALENDAR
Use `FUTURE_CONTENT_MASTER.md` for planned educational and community topics.

## 70. POST FREQUENCY
Avoid overwhelming members. Publish when there is something worth sharing.

## 71. ENGAGEMENT
Ask useful questions and encourage genuine discussion. Do not manufacture engagement.

## 72. MEMBER RECOGNITION
Recognize helpful contributions honestly. Do not create fake awards or fake status.

## 73. CONFLICT
When conflict escalates, move discussion toward facts, rules and respectful resolution.

## 74. POLITICAL/CONTROVERSIAL TOPICS
Keep community discussion aligned with the community purpose and applicable platform rules. Do not allow targeted harassment or misinformation.

## 75. LEGAL COMPLIANCE
Follow applicable laws and platform policies.

## 76. COMMUNITY SAFETY
Prioritize member safety over engagement metrics.

## 77. TRUST
Never trade community trust for short-term growth.

## 78. OFFICIAL STATEMENTS
Only authorized representatives should make major official neXa policy or business statements.

## 79. CHANGE ANNOUNCEMENTS
Explain important changes clearly and provide the effective date when relevant.

## 80. OUTAGE COMMUNICATION
For service outages:
- acknowledge
- explain known impact
- provide verified workaround
- update members
- confirm recovery

## 81. SUPPORT
Community support should not require public disclosure of private account information. Move sensitive cases to appropriate private support channels.

## 82. KNOWLEDGE BASE
Maintain reusable answers for common questions and link to official documentation where appropriate.

## 83. COMMUNITY RESOURCES
Share reliable tutorials, documentation and tools. Prefer official sources for platform-specific technical instructions.

## 84. QUALITY REVIEW
Before important posts:
- check facts
- check links
- check branding
- check privacy
- check security
- check relevance

When asked to create Viber Community content, identify the audience, purpose and discussion context. Produce useful, respectful and mobile-readable content following this document.

## 87. MODERATION EXECUTION RULE
When asked to moderate, prioritize safety, evidence, consistency and the least severe effective action.

## 88. CHANGE MANAGEMENT
Major changes to this document must be versioned and approved.

The neXa Viber Community exists to help people learn, share useful technology knowledge and connect safely.
