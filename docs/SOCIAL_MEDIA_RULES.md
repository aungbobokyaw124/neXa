neXa SOCIAL MEDIA RULES

Version: 1.0
Status: Official
Brand: neXa
Tagline: Build Faster. Create Smarter.

---

1. PURPOSE

This document defines the official social media rules for neXa.

It applies to all official and future neXa social channels, including:

- Facebook
- Telegram
- TikTok
- Viber Business
- Viber Community
- YouTube
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- Future approved platforms

Each platform may have a different content style, but all channels must remain part of the same neXa ecosystem.

---

All official channels must use:

Brand: neXa

Tagline: Build Faster. Create Smarter.

Primary: "#0B1F3A"

3. BRAND CONSISTENCY

The following must remain consistent across platforms:

- brand name
- logo
- official colors
- tagline
- tone
- core mission
- visual identity

Platform-specific design may change.

The core brand must not.

---

4. SOCIAL MEDIA PURPOSE

Social media should support one or more of the following:

1. Education
2. Technology awareness
3. Product discovery
4. Service promotion
5. Community building
6. Customer support
7. Brand awareness
8. Trust building
9. Traffic generation
10. Business growth

Every post should have a purpose.

6. CONTENT VALUE RULE

A post should provide at least one of:

- knowledge
- useful information
- solution
- inspiration
- demonstration
- entertainment with technology relevance
- product value

Avoid posting simply to maintain activity.

---

7. CONTENT QUALITY

Content must be:

- accurate
- understandable
- useful
- relevant
- honest
- visually consistent

Avoid:

- fake claims
- fabricated statistics
- misleading screenshots
- fake testimonials
- fake results
- copied content without permission

---

8. FACT-CHECKING

Before publishing information that may change over time, verify:

- platform policies
- pricing
- product availability
- software versions
- AI capabilities
- regional restrictions
- technical specifications

If information is uncertain, say so.

---

9. LANGUAGE

Primary communication may use:

- Myanmar
- English
- Myanmar + English

Choose the language based on the audience and platform.

Technical terms may remain in English when that improves clarity.

---

10. WRITING STYLE

Preferred:

- direct
- simple
- practical
- confident
- helpful

Avoid:

- unnecessary formal language
- excessive slang
- exaggerated claims
- excessive emojis
- long introductions

---

11. HEADLINE RULE

A strong headline should quickly communicate the value.

Examples:

- "Build a Website from Your Phone"
- "3 AI Tools That Save Time"
- "How to Deploy a Website with Vercel"
- "neXa Template Update"

Avoid clickbait such as:

- "YOU WON'T BELIEVE THIS!"
- "SECRET TRICK!"
- "EVERYONE IS SHOCKED!"

---

12. HOOK RULE

For short-form content, the first seconds must communicate the reason to continue.

Possible hooks:

- problem
- result
- question
- surprising fact
- demonstration
- practical tip

Do not use false hooks.

---

13. CTA RULE

Calls to action should be relevant.

Examples:

- Learn more
- Try it
- Save this
- Share with someone who needs it
- Follow neXa
- Join the community
- View the project
- Contact neXa

Do not use a CTA in every sentence.

---

14. HASHTAG RULE

Use relevant hashtags.

Avoid excessive hashtags.

Hashtags should describe:

- topic
- technology
- product
- audience
- brand

Possible brand hashtag:

"#neXa"

Do not create large numbers of meaningless hashtags.

---

15. EMOJI RULE

Emojis are optional.

Use them only when they improve readability.

Avoid:

- excessive emoji
- emoji-only communication
- random emoji decoration

---

16. IMAGE RULE

Social images should follow neXa branding.

Preferred:

- Deep Navy
- Emerald
- White
- clean typography
- clear hierarchy
- minimal layout

Avoid:

- random color combinations
- low-quality images
- unreadable text
- excessive visual effects

---

17. VIDEO RULE

Videos should prioritize:

- clear subject
- readable text
- strong opening
- good audio
- useful information
- appropriate pacing

Short-form videos should generally get to the point quickly.

---

18. WATERMARK RULE

When appropriate, use a subtle neXa brand mark.

Do not make the watermark so large that it interferes with the content.

---

19. PLATFORM ADAPTATION

Do not simply copy the exact same post to every platform.

Adapt:

- length
- format
- hook
- CTA
- hashtags
- visual dimensions
- audience expectations

The message may remain consistent.

The presentation should be platform-specific.

---

27. POSTING CONSISTENCY

Consistency is more important than excessive posting.

Do not publish low-quality content simply to maintain a schedule.

Prefer:

Useful + consistent

over:

Frequent + meaningless

---

28. CONTENT CALENDAR

A content calendar may include:

- educational posts
- product posts
- service posts
- community posts
- technical tips
- AI content
- project updates
- behind-the-scenes content

Balance promotional and educational content.

---

29. PROMOTIONAL BALANCE

Avoid turning every post into an advertisement.

A practical balance may be:

Education
Education
Value / Tip
Project
Product / Service
Community
Education

Adjust based on audience response.

---

30. COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Respond to useful comments and questions when possible.

Do not:

- argue unnecessarily
- insult users
- reveal private information
- engage in harassment
- make promises that cannot be fulfilled

---

31. CUSTOMER QUESTIONS

When answering questions:

1. Understand the question.
2. Provide the direct answer.
3. Add useful context if necessary.
4. Give the next step.

---

32. NEGATIVE COMMENTS

Do not respond emotionally.

If criticism is valid:

- acknowledge it
- correct the issue
- explain the solution

If criticism is false:

- respond with facts
- avoid unnecessary argument

---

33. SPAM POLICY

Do not use:

- repetitive comments
- mass unsolicited messages
- fake engagement
- fake followers
- misleading promotions
- automated spam

---

34. PLATFORM POLICY

All social activity must comply with the platform's current rules.

Do not attempt to bypass:

- platform restrictions
- account security
- verification systems
- payment rules
- content policies

---

35. PRIVACY

Never publicly expose:

- customer phone numbers
- private messages
- passwords
- account credentials
- private documents
- private API keys
- confidential business information

without proper authorization.

---

36. AI-GENERATED CONTENT

AI may assist with:

- ideas
- scripts
- captions
- designs
- images
- videos
- translations
- research
- editing

Human review is required before publishing important claims.

---

37. AI TRANSPARENCY

Do not falsely claim:

- human-created work when disclosure is required
- real customer results that do not exist
- real testimonials that were generated
- real photographs when they are AI-generated, where disclosure is relevant

---

38. COPYRIGHT

Do not publish copyrighted material without appropriate rights.

When using:

- images
- music
- videos
- articles
- logos
- software

ensure appropriate permission or licensing.

---

39. BRAND SAFETY

neXa content must not intentionally promote:

- scams
- fraud
- misinformation
- illegal activity
- malicious software
- harmful deception

---

40. ANALYTICS

Track useful metrics such as:

- reach
- views
- watch time
- engagement
- saves
- shares
- clicks
- conversions
- followers
- community growth

Do not judge content only by views.

---

41. CONTENT PERFORMANCE

When a post performs well:

1. Identify why.
2. Extract the useful pattern.
3. Create related content.
4. Avoid copying the same post repeatedly.

---

42. CONTENT FAILURE

When a post performs poorly:

Do not automatically delete it.

Evaluate:

- topic
- hook
- timing
- format
- audience
- thumbnail
- title
- CTA

Use the result as feedback.

---

43. SOCIAL MEDIA SECURITY

Protect all official accounts with:

- strong passwords
- two-factor authentication
- secure recovery methods
- limited admin access
- secure email
- secure API tokens

Never share credentials through public channels.

---

44. ACCOUNT STRUCTURE

Where supported, use consistent:

- profile name
- username
- profile image
- bio
- website
- contact information

Use the official neXa identity.

---

45. PROFILE BIO

The profile bio should clearly communicate:

- what neXa is
- what it provides
- what users can do next

Avoid stuffing the bio with unnecessary keywords.

---

46. LINK POLICY

Use official neXa links whenever possible.

Never publish an unverified link.

Before posting:

- open the link
- verify destination
- verify HTTPS
- verify correct page

---

47. CONTENT ARCHIVE

Important content should be preserved.

Keep source materials where practical:

- scripts
- captions
- graphics
- videos
- links
- campaign notes

---

48. MASTER CONTENT RULE

Every major content campaign should have:

Goal
Audience
Core message
Platform
Format
Hook
Main content
CTA
Publish date
Performance

---

49. SOCIAL MEDIA AI RULE

AI generating social media content must follow:

- this document
- platform-specific master prompts
- neXa brand guidelines
- neXa content policies

AI must not invent company information.

---

50. FUTURE PLATFORM RULE

When a new social platform is added:

1. Define purpose.
2. Define audience.
3. Define content type.
4. Create platform-specific rules.
5. Connect it to the neXa ecosystem.
6. Protect account security.
7. Verify availability and policies.

---

51. FINAL SOCIAL MEDIA PRINCIPLE

neXa social media should not exist simply to collect followers.

It should:

Teach.

Help.

Demonstrate.

Build trust.

Connect people.

Grow the neXa ecosystem.
