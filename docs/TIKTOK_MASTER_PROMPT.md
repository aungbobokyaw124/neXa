# neXa TIKTOK MASTER PROMPT

Version: 1.0  
Status: OFFICIAL / MASTER  
Brand: neXa  
Tagline: Build Faster. Create Smarter.

## 1. PURPOSE
Define permanent rules for neXa TikTok content, short-form video, educational clips, product demonstrations, brand awareness and community growth.

## 2. BRAND
Use `neXa` consistently. Official tagline: `Build Faster. Create Smarter.` Core colors: `#0B1F3A`, `#10B981`, `#FFFFFF`.

## 3. CONTENT GOAL
Create short videos that are useful, understandable, memorable and relevant to technology, AI, web development, automation, digital services and education.

## 4. CONTENT PRINCIPLES
Prioritize:
- value
- clarity
- authenticity
- practical demonstrations
- strong hooks without deception
- consistent branding

## 5. AUDIENCE
Content may target:
- Myanmar technology users
- beginners
- creators
- small businesses
- developers
- students
- digital-service customers
- international technology audiences

## 6. VIDEO STRUCTURE
Preferred structure:
Hook → Problem → Value/Demonstration → Result → CTA.

## 7. HOOK
The first seconds must communicate why the viewer should continue watching. Hooks must remain truthful.

## 8. NO CLICKBAIT
Do not use fake claims, fake results or misleading titles.

## 9. EDUCATIONAL CONTENT
Tutorials should show a real process where possible. Explain important steps clearly.

## 10. DEMONSTRATIONS
When showing a product, website or AI tool, demonstrate actual behavior. Do not fake functionality.

## 11. SCREEN RECORDINGS
Keep screen recordings readable. Crop unnecessary UI and highlight the important action.

## 12. MOBILE FORMAT
Default video format: vertical 9:16.

## 13. TEXT SAFE AREA
Keep important text away from TikTok interface areas such as bottom controls and top overlays.

## 14. CAPTIONS
Captions should be short, readable and synchronized with speech or important visual actions.

## 15. TYPOGRAPHY
Use a consistent modern sans-serif style. Maintain strong contrast.

## 16. VISUAL IDENTITY
Use neXa colors and visual language without making every frame visually identical.

## 17. LOGO
Use the approved neXa logo. Do not distort or randomly recolor it.

## 18. MUSIC
Use music only when licensed/available through permitted sources. Music must not overpower speech.

## 19. VOICE
Voice-over should be natural, clear and understandable. AI voice may be used when appropriate.

## 20. LANGUAGE
Myanmar content should use natural Burmese. Technical terms may remain in English when clearer. International content may use English.

## 21. VIDEO LENGTH
Choose length based on the idea. Do not stretch content merely to make a video longer.

## 22. SERIES
Create recurring series when a topic can be taught progressively.

## 23. SERIES NAMING
Series names should be consistent and easy to remember.

## 24. CONTENT PILLARS
Recommended pillars:
- AI
- Web Development
- Coding
- Automation
- Cloud
- Digital Tools
- Tutorials
- Troubleshooting
- neXa Products
- Digital Business

## 25. POST FREQUENCY
Consistency is valuable, but quality comes first. Avoid repetitive low-value posts.

## 26. CTA
Use one primary CTA when possible:
- Follow neXa
- Learn More
- Try it
- Comment your question
- Visit the official neXa link

## 27. COMMENTS
Use comments as a source of content ideas. Respond respectfully and correct misinformation.

## 28. COMMUNITY
Do not manipulate engagement through fake comments, fake accounts or fake testimonials.

## 29. HASHTAGS
Use a small number of relevant hashtags. Avoid irrelevant trending hashtags.

## 30. TREND USAGE
Use trends only when they fit neXa's subject and brand. Do not chase every trend.

## 31. TREND VERIFICATION
Current TikTok features, trends and limits change. Verify current information before making claims about them.

## 32. CONTENT RESEARCH
Research the topic before publishing technical or current-affairs claims.

## 33. FACT CHECKING
Separate verified facts from personal opinion or demonstration results.

## 34. NO FAKE RESULTS
Never claim an AI tool, product or workflow achieved a result that was not actually tested or verified.

## 35. PRODUCT PROMOTION
Promotional videos should explain real benefits and relevant limitations.

## 36. PRICING
Verify current pricing before publishing it.

## 37. FREE CLAIMS
Do not say `free forever` unless the provider explicitly guarantees it.

## 38. TUTORIAL SAFETY
Warn viewers before destructive actions such as deleting files, changing production settings or exposing credentials.

## 39. SECURITY
Never show API keys, passwords, tokens, private URLs or personal information in a recording.

## 40. SCREEN PRIVACY
Blur or hide sensitive notifications, emails, phone numbers and account identifiers.

## 41. AI TOOLS
When presenting AI tools, accurately identify the model/service and explain important limitations.

## 42. PROMPT CONTENT
Prompts shown publicly must not contain secrets or private customer data.

## 43. SOURCE MATERIAL
Use original, licensed or platform-permitted media.

## 44. COPYRIGHT
Do not reuse copyrighted content in ways that violate applicable rights or platform rules.

## 45. THUMBNAILS
Thumbnail text should be short and accurately represent the video.

## 46. COVER DESIGN
Use a consistent neXa visual language for profile and series covers.

## 47. STORYTELLING
A technical topic can be presented as:
Problem → Attempt → Discovery → Solution → Result.

## 48. BEFORE/AFTER
Before/after demonstrations must represent real states and real changes.

## 49. CASE STUDIES
Case studies must be based on genuine projects or clearly labeled examples.

## 50. USER TESTIMONIALS
Use real testimonials only with appropriate permission. Never invent testimonials.

## 51. CONTENT CALENDAR
Plan content using `FUTURE_CONTENT_MASTER.md`.

## 52. REPURPOSING
Adapt long-form content into short videos rather than copying text unchanged.

## 53. FACEBOOK CROSS-POSTING
A TikTok video may be adapted for Facebook Reels, but captions and CTA should be platform-appropriate.

## 54. TELEGRAM CROSS-POSTING
Use Telegram for deeper explanations, links and resources referenced by TikTok videos.

## 55. VIBER CROSS-POSTING
Adapt important announcements and offers for Viber rather than blindly reposting TikTok captions.

## 56. ANALYTICS
Where analytics are available, monitor:
- views
- retention
- completion
- engagement
- follows
- clicks

## 57. ANALYTICS INTERPRETATION
Do not treat one viral video as proof of a permanent strategy. Look for patterns across multiple posts.

## 58. A/B TESTING
Test one meaningful variable at a time where practical:
- hook
- opening visual
- caption
- CTA
- duration

## 59. RETENTION
Improve retention through useful pacing, not deceptive editing.

## 60. EDITING
Remove unnecessary pauses, dead space and irrelevant footage.

## 61. PACING
Match pacing to the subject. Technical tutorials may need slower pacing than entertainment clips.

## 62. B-ROLL
Use B-roll when it improves understanding. Do not add random footage merely for decoration.

## 63. SCREEN HIGHLIGHTS
Use subtle highlights, zooms or arrows to guide attention.

## 64. AUDIO QUALITY
Prioritize clear speech and clean audio over complicated effects.

## 65. VISUAL QUALITY
Aim for clear, sharp and stable footage. Avoid unnecessary filters.

## 66. AI-GENERATED VISUALS
AI visuals may be used when clearly appropriate. Do not use them to falsely represent real events or real people.

## 67. DISCLOSURE
When required by platform rules or law, disclose synthetic or altered media appropriately.

## 68. COMMENT MODERATION
Hide/remove spam, scams, harassment and dangerous misinformation according to platform tools and applicable rules.

## 69. ACCOUNT SECURITY
Use strong account security and two-factor authentication where available.

## 70. ADMIN ACCESS
Only trusted people should have account management access.

## 71. LINK SECURITY
Use official neXa links and verified destinations. Do not publish suspicious shortened links.

## 72. BIO
The profile bio should clearly explain what neXa does and provide the primary official destination.

## 73. PROFILE CONSISTENCY
Profile name, logo, bio and link should remain consistent with the official neXa identity.

## 74. BRAND EVOLUTION
Do not change the core brand identity casually. Major changes require approval and documentation.

## 75. CONTENT REVIEW
Before publishing, check:
- facts
- spelling
- audio
- captions
- branding
- links
- privacy
- security

## 76. PUBLISHING WORKFLOW
Idea → research → script → record/generate → edit → review → caption → publish → monitor.

## 77. SCRIPT RULE
Scripts should sound natural when spoken. Avoid overly formal text unless the topic requires it.

## 78. SCRIPT TEMPLATE
```text
HOOK:
[Why should the viewer watch?]

PROBLEM:
[What problem exists?]

VALUE:
[What will the viewer learn?]

DEMO:
[Show the actual process]

RESULT:
[Show the verified outcome]

CTA:
[One useful next step]
```

## 79. EDUCATION SERIES
For beginner education, start simple and progressively increase difficulty.

## 80. TECHNICAL ACCURACY
If uncertain about a technical claim, verify it before publication.

## 81. CURRENT PLATFORM FEATURES
TikTok features, monetization rules, regional availability and limits may change. Verify current information before publishing.

## 82. MONETIZATION
Do not promise monetization eligibility or income. Explain that eligibility depends on current platform rules and region.

## 83. BUSINESS CLAIMS
Do not promise guaranteed revenue, customers, rankings or viral results.

## 84. ETHICS
Do not use deceptive engagement tactics, fake scarcity, fake social proof or impersonation.

## 85. USER SAFETY
Do not encourage dangerous technical experiments without appropriate warnings and context.

## 86. POST TEMPLATE
```text
[HOOK]

[VALUE / EXPLANATION]

[KEY POINTS]

[RESULT]

[CTA]

#neXa #Technology
```

## 87. AI EXECUTION RULE
When asked to create TikTok content, identify audience, topic, goal, format and platform constraints before producing the final script/caption when those details matter.

## 88. CHANGE MANAGEMENT
Keep this document versioned. Major changes should be reviewed and documented.

## 89. FINAL CHECKLIST
- [ ] Hook is truthful
- [ ] Content is useful
- [ ] Branding is correct
- [ ] Captions readable
- [ ] 9:16 layout checked
- [ ] No secrets visible
- [ ] No private data visible
- [ ] Facts checked
- [ ] Links checked
- [ ] CTA is truthful
- [ ] Copyright considered

## 90. MASTER TIKTOK PROMPT
Create TikTok content for neXa that is useful, truthful, mobile-first and engaging without deception. Follow the official neXa branding and current project rules. Prioritize practical technology, AI, web development, automation, cloud, digital services and education. Never fabricate results, testimonials, partnerships or platform claims.

## 91. PERMANENT PRINCIPLE
TikTok is a discovery and education channel for neXa. Every video should provide a reason for the viewer to watch and a useful reason to remember neXa.

## 92. FINAL RULE
**Build Faster. Create Smarter.**

Create useful content. Protect users. Protect the brand. Never trade trust for views.
